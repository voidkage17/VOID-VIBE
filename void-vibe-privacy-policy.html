01 / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Void Vibe is a Discord music bot designed to support music playback, queue management, and related commands. We aim to process only the information reasonably needed to provide, secure, and troubleshoot these features.

Information that may be processed

  • Discord identifiers: user, server, and channel IDs needed to respond to commands and manage playback.
  • Music requests: search terms, track URLs, and playback metadata needed to find and play music.
  • Voice state: information needed to connect the bot to a voice channel and maintain playback.
  • Operational diagnostics: error messages and technical logs used to troubleshoot crashes or service issues.

The actual information processed depends on the features enabled in the deployed version. Do not treat this list as confirmation that every item is collected or stored.

How information is used

  • Respond to commands and operate music playback.
  • Manage queues, playback state, and requested tracks.
  • Maintain service reliability, investigate errors, and prevent abuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations where required.

Sharing

Void Vibe does not need to sell personal information to provide its core music features. Information may pass through Discord, the Lavalink server, hosting providers, or music-source integrations as necessary to operate the service. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. Disclosures may also occur where required by law.

Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect bot tokens, API credentials, logs, and any stored information. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. Never publish secrets in Discord messages, source code, screenshots, or public repositories.

02 / Data

Information, Storage & Retention

Temporary processing

Music queues and playback state may be held temporarily in memory while the bot is running. They may be cleared when playback ends, the bot disconnects, or the process restarts, depending on the implementation.

Logs and persistent storage

Technical logs or persistent records may be kept if the deployed version is configured to create them. The bot owner should specify what is stored, where it is stored, and the retention period before publishing this policy. Do not claim that data is never stored unless the code and hosting setup have been checked.

Your choices

If you have a privacy question or believe the bot has retained information about you, contact the bot owner using the details in the Contact section. Requests will be handled under applicable law and may be subject to legitimate technical or legal retention requirements.

03 / Integrations

Third-Party Services

Void Vibe may rely on Discord, Lavalink, hosting infrastructure, and configured music-source plugins or platforms. Music availability, metadata, and playback can depend on these external services.

  • External services may process requests or technical information under their own policies.
  • Some links may be unsupported, unavailable, private, region-restricted, or removed.
  • Spotify or Apple Music links may require metadata matching against a supported playback source and may not provide direct audio playback.
  • Void Vibe does not claim ownership of third-party music or other copyrighted content.

Users must follow the applicable terms and laws for any third-party service they use.

Service features

Void Vibe may offer commands for playing, pausing, resuming, skipping, stopping, queuing, shuffling, looping, and controlling music volume. Features can change as the project develops.

Music and copyright

Users are responsible for ensuring their use of music and third-party platforms complies with applicable laws and platform terms. Void Vibe does not guarantee that a particular track or source will remain available.

Changes to the service

The bot owner may update, suspend, or discontinue features, integrations, or access where reasonably necessary. We will aim to update this page when changes materially affect these terms or privacy practices.

05 / Community

Acceptable Use

When using Void Vibe, you agree not to:

  • Abuse commands, exploit vulnerabilities, or intentionally disrupt the service.
  • Attempt unauthorized access to the bot, its hosting environment, credentials, or infrastructure.
  • Use the bot to facilitate unlawful activity or infringe third-party rights.

Access may be limited where necessary to protect the bot, its users, or its infrastructure.
